_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

**What we collect.** Money Starter has no accounts, comments or sign-up forms, so we do not collect your name, email or other personal details directly.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Cookies and advertising.** Some pages may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

**Affiliate links.** When you click an affiliate link, the retailer (for example Amazon) may set a cookie to record that you came from this site. Their own privacy policies apply.

**Purchases.** Purchases of the Money Starter Kit are handled by Gumroad, under [Gumroad's privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy). We never see your card details.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
