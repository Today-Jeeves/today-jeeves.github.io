Money Starter publishes plain-English guides for people who want to get a handle on their money: making a budget that actually holds up, paying off debt in the right order, and saving for goals without guessing.

**What you can expect from every guide**

- A straight answer near the top, then the detail.
- Worked examples with real numbers you can check yourself.
- No hype, no "get rich" promises, and no pressure to buy anything.

**How the guides are made.** Articles are drafted with the help of AI writing tools and checked against primary sources such as the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), the IRS and the FDIC. Calculations in examples are run in a spreadsheet before they are published. If you spot a mistake, it gets fixed.

**How the site earns money.** We sell the [Money Starter Kit]({{ site.kit_url }}), a set of budgeting, debt payoff and savings spreadsheets. Some pages may also contain affiliate links or ads. See the full [disclosure](/disclosure/).

**Not advice.** Everything here is general education. Your situation is your own, so check the details with a qualified professional before making big decisions.
